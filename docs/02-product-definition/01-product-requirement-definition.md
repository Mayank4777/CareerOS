# Purpose

This Product Requirements Document (PRD) defines the overall product vision, scope, objectives, capabilities, and functional requirements for CareerOS.

It serves as the primary reference for product management, design, engineering, testing, and AI coding agents throughout development.

This document intentionally focuses on **what** the product must achieve rather than **how** it will be implemented.

---

# Product Overview

CareerOS is a Career Operating System designed for early-career software professionals.

Rather than solving one isolated problem such as resume creation or application tracking, CareerOS continuously helps users:

- Organize career information.
- Understand career progress.
- Identify improvement opportunities.
- Make better career decisions.

CareerOS acts as the intelligence layer connecting fragmented career activities into one continuous experience.

---

# Vision

Empower people to build successful careers through a unified system that understands their journey, identifies opportunities, and guides their next best step.

---

# Mission

Help students and early-career software professionals organize, understand, and continuously improve their career journey through personalized guidance.

---

# Target Users

Primary users include:

- Final-year Computer Science students
- Information Technology students
- Recent graduates
- Internship seekers
- Entry-level software engineers

The MVP does not target experienced professionals or recruiters.

---

# Product Goals

CareerOS should enable users to:

- Organize career information.
- Reduce manual tracking.
- Understand employability.
- Identify weaknesses.
- Improve continuously.
- Prepare effectively.
- Increase confidence.
- Make better career decisions.

---

# Problems Being Solved

CareerOS addresses the following problems:

- Fragmented career information.
- Lack of personalized guidance.
- Manual application tracking.
- Limited visibility into career progress.
- Repeated application mistakes.
- Poor understanding of employability.
- Difficulty identifying next actions.

---

# Product Principles

The product follows these principles:

- Solve validated problems.
- Reduce user effort.
- Guide rather than overwhelm.
- Build around the user's journey.
- Transform data into understanding.
- Personalize recommendations.
- Build trust through transparency.
- Respect user ownership of data.
- Use AI as an assistant.
- Prioritize focus over feature quantity.

---

# Core Product Capabilities

CareerOS is built around three continuous capabilities.

---

## Collect

CareerOS collects career information from user-controlled sources with minimal manual effort.

Examples include:

- Resume uploads.
- Browser-assisted job capture.
- GitHub repositories.
- Manual additions.
- Future integrations.

---

## Understand

CareerOS analyzes collected information to understand:

- Career readiness.
- Skill gaps.
- Resume evolution.
- Application trends.
- Interview history.
- Career progress.

---

## Guide

CareerOS provides personalized guidance by recommending:

- Next best actions.
- Skill priorities.
- Resume improvements.
- Interview preparation.
- Career insights.

# Non-Functional Requirements

CareerOS should provide:

- Simple user experience.
- Fast performance.
- Secure user data.
- Reliable synchronization.
- Scalable architecture.
- Responsive interface.
- Clear explanations for AI recommendations.

---

# Success Metrics

Success will be measured through:

- User retention.
- Active usage.
- Reduced manual effort.
- Recommendation engagement.
- Career profile completeness.
- User satisfaction.
- Progress toward career goals.

---

# Out of Scope (MVP)

The MVP will not include:

- Recruiter tools.
- Corporate hiring.
- University administration.
- Social networking.
- Internal messaging.
- Learning platform replacement.
- Guaranteed job placement.

---

# Product Success Definition

CareerOS succeeds when users consistently understand:

- Where they are today.
- What is preventing progress.
- What they should improve next.
- How their career is evolving.
