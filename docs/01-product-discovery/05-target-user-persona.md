# Purpose

This document defines the primary users CareerOS is designed for during the MVP phase.

The goal is to ensure product decisions are made for a clearly defined audience instead of trying to satisfy every type of job seeker.

This persona is based on behavioral patterns and user needs rather than demographic information alone.

---

# Primary Persona

## Early-Career Software Job Seeker

CareerOS is initially designed for students and recent graduates preparing for software engineering internships and entry-level jobs.

These users are highly motivated but often lack structure, visibility, and personalized guidance throughout the hiring process.

---

# Profile

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| Career Stage | Final-year student or recent graduate |
| Education | Computer Science, Information Technology, Software Engineering, or related field |
| Experience | 0–2 years |
| Target Roles | Software Engineer, Backend Developer, Frontend Developer, Full Stack Developer, AI/ML Engineer, Data Analyst (entry level), QA Engineer |
| Job Search | Active |
| Technical Comfort | High |
| Digital Literacy | High |

---

# Goals

The primary goals of this persona include:

- Secure a software internship or full-time job.
- Improve employability.
- Build skills demanded by employers.
- Understand why previous applications failed.
- Track career progress.
- Prepare effectively for interviews.
- Avoid wasting time on low-impact activities.

---

# Behaviors

Typical behaviors include:

- Applies through multiple job portals.
- Maintains multiple resume versions.
- Uses GitHub to showcase projects.
- Learns from YouTube, ChatGPT, documentation, and online courses.
- Frequently modifies resumes before applying.
- Tracks applications inconsistently or not at all.
- Searches online for interview experiences.
- Participates in hackathons, coding contests, or technical communities.

---

# Pain Points

The persona commonly experiences the following problems:

## Fragmented Information

Career information exists across many different platforms with no unified view.

---

## Lack of Direction

The user knows they need to improve but does not know what should be prioritized.

---

## No Visibility Into Progress

The user cannot easily determine whether they are becoming more employable over time.

---

## Repeated Mistakes

The same resume, application strategy, or interview preparation methods are often repeated despite poor outcomes.

---

## Manual Tracking Fatigue

Maintaining spreadsheets or manually recording every application quickly becomes frustrating and is often abandoned.

---

## Limited Feedback

Most companies provide little or no explanation for application rejections.

---

# Motivations

This persona values products that help them:

- Save time.
- Stay organized.
- Receive personalized guidance.
- Improve interview success.
- Increase confidence.
- Understand measurable career progress.

---

# Technology Usage

Typical tools already used include:

- LinkedIn
- Naukri
- Indeed
- Internshala
- GitHub
- Gmail
- ChatGPT
- Google Docs
- Google Drive
- Notion
- YouTube
- Coding platforms (LeetCode, HackerRank, Codeforces)

CareerOS should integrate naturally into this existing workflow rather than replace these tools.

---

# User Expectations

The user expects CareerOS to:

- Reduce manual effort.
- Capture career information with minimal friction.
- Organize career history automatically.
- Provide actionable recommendations instead of generic advice.
- Show measurable progress over time.
- Help identify the next best action.

---

# Success Criteria

This persona considers CareerOS successful when they can confidently answer:

- Am I improving?
- What should I do next?
- Why am I not getting interviews?
- Which opportunities fit my profile?
- Where am I losing opportunities?
- What actions will most improve my chances of getting hired?

---

# Design Implications

This persona influences several product decisions:

- Automation should be preferred over manual data entry wherever possible.
- Browser-assisted job capture should minimize repetitive work.
- Insights should be simple, actionable, and evidence-based.
- The dashboard should focus on next actions rather than raw data.
- Every major feature should reduce cognitive load rather than add complexity.

---

# Out of Scope (MVP)

The initial MVP is **not** optimized for:

- Experienced professionals with 5+ years of experience.
- Executive or leadership hiring.
- Non-technical careers.
- Recruitment agencies.
- Hiring managers.
- Corporate HR teams.

Support for these audiences may be explored after successful validation of the initial target market.

---

# Persona Summary

The ideal MVP user is a technically capable student or recent graduate who actively applies for software roles but lacks a unified system to organize career information, understand progress, and continuously improve employability.

CareerOS exists to provide that missing system.
