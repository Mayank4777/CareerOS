**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Approved  
**Last Updated:** July 28, 2026

---

# Purpose

The Vision Statement defines the long-term future that CareerOS aims to create.

It is the product's guiding principle and should remain stable even as features, technology, business models, and implementation details evolve.

Every major product decision should be evaluated against this vision.

---

# Vision

> **To empower people to build successful careers through a unified system that understands their journey, identifies their opportunities, and guides their next best step.**

---

# What This Means

CareerOS is not intended to replace job portals, resume builders, or professional networking platforms.

Instead, CareerOS serves as the intelligent operating system that connects a user's career information across multiple platforms and provides personalized guidance throughout their career journey.

Rather than helping users simply apply for more jobs, CareerOS aims to help them make better career decisions.

---

# Long-Term Vision

As CareerOS evolves, it should become the trusted career companion that continuously understands:

- Current skills
- Career goals
- Resume evolution
- Job applications
- Interview history
- Recruiter interactions
- Learning progress
- Career achievements

Using this accumulated knowledge, CareerOS will provide personalized recommendations that help users progress toward their career goals.

---

# Guiding Principles

CareerOS should always strive to:

- Provide clarity instead of overwhelming users with information.
- Recommend actionable next steps instead of generic advice.
- Learn continuously from each user's career journey.
- Integrate with existing career platforms instead of replacing them.
- Use AI as an enhancement layer rather than making AI the product itself.

---

# Decision Filter

Every future feature should answer the following questions:

1. Does this help users better understand their current career position?
2. Does this help users move closer to their career goals?
3. Does this help users decide what to do next?
4. Does this align with the long-term vision of becoming a unified career operating system?

If the answer to these questions is "No," the feature should be reconsidered before development.

---

# Out of Scope

The vision of CareerOS is **not** to become:

- A traditional job portal
- A standalone resume builder
- A LinkedIn replacement
- A generic AI chatbot
- A collection of unrelated productivity tools

These may exist as supporting capabilities, but they are not the primary purpose of the product.

---

# Vision Summary

CareerOS exists to help people make better career decisions by becoming the single system that understands their professional journey and guides them toward meaningful career growth.
