# Jobs To Be Done (JTBD)

**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Approved  
**Last Updated:** July 28, 2026

---

# Purpose

This document identifies the core jobs users expect CareerOS to perform.

Unlike features, Jobs To Be Done focus on the outcomes users want to achieve.

Every major feature should exist because it helps users complete one or more of these jobs.

If a proposed feature does not support a JTBD, it should be reconsidered before development.

---

# Primary Job

## Help me become employable and secure a software job by continuously showing me what I should improve next.

This is the core job CareerOS is hired to perform.

Everything else supports this objective.

---

# Functional Jobs

## JTBD-01

### Organize My Career

**When**

My career information is scattered across multiple platforms.

**I want to**

Keep everything in one place.

**So I can**

Understand my complete career journey without switching between multiple tools.

---

## JTBD-02

### Track My Applications

**When**

I apply to many jobs every week.

**I want to**

Automatically capture and organize my applications.

**So I can**

Focus on applying and improving instead of maintaining spreadsheets.

---

## JTBD-03

### Understand My Progress

**When**

I have been applying for weeks or months.

**I want to**

See whether I am actually improving.

**So I can**

Avoid repeating ineffective strategies.

---

## JTBD-04

### Identify My Weaknesses

**When**

I receive few interview calls or repeated rejections.

**I want to**

Understand what is preventing me from getting hired.

**So I can**

Focus on improvements that matter.

---

## JTBD-05

### Prioritize My Learning

**When**

There are hundreds of technologies and courses available.

**I want to**

Know what skills are most valuable for my target role.

**So I can**

Spend my learning time effectively.

---

## JTBD-06

### Prepare With Context

**When**

I receive an interview invitation.

**I want to**

Access everything related to that opportunity in one place.

**So I can**

Prepare more effectively.

---

## JTBD-07

### Learn From Every Application

**When**

An application or interview ends.

**I want to**

Record outcomes and feedback.

**So I can**

Continuously improve future performance.

# Emotional Jobs

Users also hire CareerOS to help them feel:

- More confident.
- Less overwhelmed.
- Better organized.
- More in control of their career.
- Less uncertain about what to do next.

---

# Social Jobs

Users also want to:

- Present themselves professionally.
- Feel competitive with peers.
- Demonstrate continuous improvement.
- Build confidence before interviews.

---

# Jobs CareerOS Will NOT Do (MVP)

CareerOS is not responsible for:

- Finding every available job.
- Guaranteeing employment.
- Completing interviews on behalf of users.
- Automatically passing ATS systems.
- Replacing recruiters or career mentors.

Its responsibility is to help users make better decisions throughout their career journey.

# Prioritization

The MVP should prioritize jobs in the following order:

1. Organize career information.
2. Reduce manual tracking.
3. Help users understand progress.
4. Identify improvement opportunities.
5. Recommend the next best action.

Jobs with the greatest impact on employability should always take precedence over convenience features.

---

# Design Implications

This document influences several product decisions:

- Every feature must support at least one JTBD.
- Automation is preferred whenever it reduces repetitive work.
- Dashboards should emphasize decisions rather than raw data.
- AI should explain why recommendations are made.
- User workflows should minimize context switching.
- Success should be measured by improved decision-making, not feature usage.

---

# Summary

Users do not hire CareerOS because it has AI, dashboards, or tracking.

They hire CareerOS because it helps them become more employable by organizing their career journey, identifying what matters most, and guiding them toward the next best action.
